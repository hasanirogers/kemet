The `border` style utility allows you to add borders to elements. It supports various border styles, widths, roundedness, and colors. Here's an example configuation for the API.


<CodePreview>
  <div data-kemet="border:success border:1 border:rounded-lg padding:xl">
    <span>The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.</span>
  </div>
</CodePreview>

[Use Storybook to generate configuations](https://storybook.kemet.dev/?path=/story/styles-api-border--standard) and to get a better understanding of the syntax.
