## Working with slots

The hard has multiple slots that allow you to add content to specific parts of the card.

### Header slot

Use the `header` slot to add content to the header of the card.

<CodePreview code='<kemet-card><div slot="header">Header</div>Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit, sed do eiusmod tempor incididunt ut labore et dolore magna aliqua.</kemet-card>' language="html" />

### Footer slot

Use the `footer` slot to add content to the footer of the card.

<CodePreview code='<kemet-card><div slot="footer">Footer</div>Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit, sed do eiusmod tempor incididunt ut labore et dolore magna aliqua.</kemet-card>' language="html" />

### Media slot

You can add images, videos, or even iframe embeds to the media slot.

<CodePreview code='<kemet-card><iframe slot="media" width="100%" style="aspect-ratio:16/9;" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/5gBqTXctxO8" title="YouTube video player" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture;" allowfullscreen></iframe>Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit, sed do eiusmod tempor incididunt ut labore et dolore magna aliqua.</kemet-card>' language="html" />

### Caption slot

You can add captions to the media slot.

<CodePreview code='<kemet-card><img slot="media" src="https://placehold.co/1920x1080" alt="a placeholder" /><div slot="caption">My awesome caption!</div>Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit, sed do eiusmod tempor incididunt ut labore et dolore magna aliqua.</kemet-card>' language="html" />
